# aTDS paper

`atds_paper.html` is the paper (published privately as an artifact). It loads
`figures/figdata.json` (aggregates, committed) and `examples.json` (worked-example traces from the
Bashan-2012 cohort and Garmin runs; generated by `scripts/examples.py`, deliberately NOT committed
because it contains short raw physiological traces). Without `examples.json` Figures 2 and 9 show a
note instead of the traces.

Plan and pre-registered hypotheses: `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`. Scripts: `scripts/`. Results: `results/`.
Commands: see Appendix A of the paper.
